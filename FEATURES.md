# Lumen — Feature Tracker

A concise, living overview of shipped capabilities and upcoming additions for **Lumen** (`Windows 10 & 11`).

## Implemented (`v1.0+`)

### Island Shell & Window Engine

- **Day Arc + Living Glow Home Page (`640×312`, `page 0`):** Compact glanceable home overview featuring a responsive parabolic solar/moon Day Arc (`Fajr–Isha` day arc with surface-masked 8-ray golden Sun glyph shifting warmer at dawn/dusk & `Isha–Fajr` moon night arc with luminous 2-star Crescent Moon glyph, compact overlap-free top-left next-prayer countdown, Friday `Jumu'ah`, Ramadan Sehri/Iftar markers, de Casteljau amber progress glow, green checkmark passed-prayer indicators, inline 5-prayer quick strip, dome-centered **Tasbih** & auto-tracked **Qibla** compass chips, interactive city switcher, overlapping event lane bars, hover time scrubber, `Full day / Next 3 hours` zoom toggle, and keyboard marker navigation), three radial transform-only Living Ambient Glow layers (time-of-day dawn/day/dusk/night, contrast-guarded album art accent, Focus session desaturation, low-battery `< 10%` edge alert, plus Reduce Transparency solid fallback), 7 native quick toggles (`Snipping Tool` screenshot, `Screen recording`, one-click `Focus timer`, `Mic` mute, `Camera` on/off, `Lock PC`, and `Silent mode`), 3 hardware-light glass bento cards (`Music` unified Home player syncing both online browser streams and offline desktop video/audio players with interactive Seek bar or `LIVE / PAUSED` indicator for non-seekable players, source session switcher pill, inline Mute/Volume slider + wheel HUD, and `Resume / Open Spotify` fallback; `Next` standard `h/m` and `<60 min` color-coded countdown linking to **Calendar Hub**; `Water` `+250 ml` tap, animated liquid-wave vessel & long-press `150/250/500 ml` picker), and optional zero-jitter status chips (`Focus timer`, `Time ledger`, auto-masked & 30s auto-cleared `Clipboard OTP`, and home-visible `Dev pulse`)
- **Calendar Hub Page (`640×312`, bottom radius `32`, `page 1` right after Home — identical footprint to Home with Smooth Slide + Crossfade paging):** Unified calendar, task, reminder, and focus workspace (`src/calendar/`) replacing the retired Waqt Timeline and consolidating the former To-do, Calendar agenda, Timed reminders, and Pomodoro pages with automatic zero-loss SQLite & settings migration. Features a 3-column (`170 / 256 / 170`, gap `8`) translucent glass layout over ambient indigo/emerald/amber glow: centered **Month** (`S–S` or Saturday-first in Bangla/locale) and 7-column **Week** strip views with color-coded day marks (`Task`, `Event`, `Reminder`, `Deadline`, `4+` overflow `+`, plus optional color-blind shape marks), day click filtering (`Back to today`), drag-and-drop or keyboard `M` rescheduling onto calendar days or the Focus card, **To-do** card with inline Quick-Add (`Ctrl+N`), natural English & Banglish date/time parsing (`quickAddParser.ts`), and 5s undo toast, **Upcoming** calendar card with `Join` meeting CTAs & event details sheet (`EventSheet`), **Reminders** card supporting one-time, daily/weekly/monthly, and prayer-anchored (`Fajr–Isha` before/after) alerts with exact-time alert overlays (`10 min` Snooze / Complete), **Calendar Sync** (`lumen-sync` supporting Local offline cache `-14d..+60d`, `ICS` / `webcal://` subscriptions, BYO Client ID `Google Calendar` OAuth PKCE, and `Microsoft Outlook / Graph` `calendarView/delta`, storing tokens strictly in Windows Credential Manager via `keyring` with `5 min` auto-sync, disconnect, and `Delete all calendar data`), and **Focus Flow** (`lumen-core::focus_engine`) with configurable `25/5/15` × `4` rounds, prayer-arrival handling (`Ask` / `Auto-pause` / `Ignore`), DND & Time Ledger sync, Free Focus post-session task logging, Lumi body-double companion states, and dedicated UI sound feedback (`day_select`, `drop_on_day`, `focus_start`, `focus_end`)
- **System & Shelf Page (`640×312`, bottom radius `32`, `page 2` right after Calendar Hub — identical footprint to Home & Calendar Hub with Smooth Slide + Crossfade paging):** Replaced the retired Control Center, standalone Water Tracker, and standalone File Shelf pages with a unified 3-column (`180 / 236 / 180`, gap `8`) OLED glass bento workspace (`src/systemShelf/`) while keeping `WaterCard` on Home (`page 0`). Features a left **File Shelf** card with a rectangular drag-drop & quick-paste dropzone, native Windows multi-file picker (`IFileOpenDialog`), OS drag-drop auto-expand onto Page 3 (`getCurrentWebview().onDragDropEvent`), 1-click shell open, path copy, missing-file badges, remove, and clear (cap `50`); a center **System** telemetry card (`lumen-windows::SystemTelemetrySampler`, visibility-gated at `1 Hz` only while expanded on Page 3) tracking CPU % & logical cores (`GetSystemTimes`), RAM GB/% (`GlobalMemoryStatusEx`), GPU utilization % + adapter name + VRAM (PDH `\GPU Engine` + `DXGI`), and Download/Upload speeds + session totals + Online/Offline status (`GetIfTable2`) with 20-point SVG sparklines and high-load amber glow alerts (`CPU >= 85%` / `GPU >= 90%`); and a right **Clipboard** history card (`lumen-storage::clipboard_history` + `lumen-windows` classifier) indexing links, code snippets, color swatches, text, pinning, 1-click re-copy, and memory-only masked OTP/API secrets that auto-wipe after `30 s` without ever writing to SQLite
- **Synthesized Soft UI Sound Engine (`lumen-media::sounds`):** Lazy Win32 `waveOut` waveform synthesizer (`5 s` idle auto-close) covering interface clicks, water droplets, chime reminders, Calendar Hub interactions (`day_select`, `drop_on_day`, `focus_start`, `focus_end`), and rate-limited `15 min` arc scrub ticks; automatically silenced during active calls/mic, fullscreen apps, Focus sessions, Windows mute, and quiet hours
- **20+17+8 State Visual QA Gallery & Lumi Character Sheet (`/dev/states`, `/dev/lumi`):** Dedicated preview matrices covering all 20 Home states, all 17 Calendar Hub states, all 8 System & Shelf states (`default`, `empty`, `high-load`, `secret-clipboard`, `offline`, `reduce-transparency`, `bangla-locale`, and `150%` scale `960×468`), and Lumi's full character sheet (all 12 moods, 3 LOD tiers across `18/22/28/56/96 px`, 4 glow hues `dusk`/`focus`/`prayer`/`night`, seasonal accessories, and touch/beat/look controls) across English & Bangla locales
- **Living Lumi Mascot System (`src/design/lumi/`, Phases 1–3):** Original light-droplet SVG + Motion state machine mascot replacing legacy faces across resting pill (`18 px` micro LOD), action/event hero (`28 px` compact LOD), and Calendar Hub Focus Flow (`42 px` full LOD with headphones & optional off-by-default seasonal accessories: Ramadan lantern, Eid sparkle, Boishakh hat). Features ambient `4s` breathing, `2.4s` spark flicker, randomized `4–7s` natural blinks (`10%` double-blink preserved under `reducedMotion`), cursor-tracking pupils (`±6 px`), priority arbitration (`calm > focus > worried > others`) with `1.5s` dwell, touch reactions (`click` squish + `happy`, `2s` hover purr arcs, `double-click` `love`), rate-limited beat nods (`<= 3 nods/s`), single active animation slot lock, bilingual (`en`/`bn`) speech bubbles (`<= 8 words`, 10-item anti-repeat, `1 tip/hr` cap, `role="status"` accessibility), strict silence rules (`calm` pose with zero bubbles/sounds during calls/mic, fullscreen apps, Focus sessions, prayer windows, and bedtime/quiet hours), Settings controls (`Lumi Mascot`, `Talk less`, `Lumi Chirp Sounds` off by default, seasonal accessories), and soft `<= 30%`-volume synthesized `waveOut` chirps (`lumi_hello`, `lumi_happy`)
- Pure-black (`#000000`) continuous **Notch** (`r=32` Home, Calendar Hub & System & Shelf / `r=24` plugin pages) and **Floating Pill** bezel surfaces with critically-damped Motion springs (`snappy`, `smooth`, `bouncy`, `gentle`, `pageSwitch`) and uniform `1.5`-stroke SVG line icons (`src/design/icons.tsx`, `src/design/motion.ts`)
- **Zero-Resize Fixed Transparent Canvas (`1040×510`):** Accommodates `150%` expanded pages (`960×468`) + margin without OS window resizes, backed by dynamic 60 Hz Win32 `WS_EX_TRANSPARENT` hit-test click pass-through and pure DirectComposition alpha compositing (no GDI `SetWindowRgn` polygon corner clipping artifacts) plus Auto scale (`<= 1920 -> 100%`, `2560+ -> 115%`, `3840+ -> 130%`)
- **Camera & Mic Privacy Dots & Unified Alert Engine:** Live Windows `ConsentStore` green/orange sensor dots in the notch alongside SQLite-backed Calendar Hub reminders, editing focus lock preventing accidental auto-collapse, exact-time repeating alert overlays (`380×108`) with Complete and `10 min` Snooze actions feeding back into Calendar Hub
- **Universal Windows & Browser Notification Sync with Real App Logos:** Real-time Windows notification pipeline (`lumen-notify` via `UserNotificationListener` + safe read-only `wpndatabase.db` snapshot) capturing live desktop and web-browser (`Chrome`, `Edge`, `Firefox`, `Brave`, WhatsApp, Discord, etc.) toasts along with Win32 `shell:AppsFolder` 48×48 PNG icon extraction, tight content-fitted dynamic sizing (`248×58` up to `428×138`) with inline **More / Show less** full-message reader (`460×260`), compact inner bottom aurora rim glow (`ActionGlowLayer` inside the notch silhouette across every state), and configurable auto-dismiss duration (`5–15s`)
- **Centered-Clock Resting Notch, Action Lumi & Audio-Reactive Music Glow:** Default `184×30` resting notch displays a centered clock flanked by real-time left indicators (Camera green dot via `KEY_READ`, live WASAPI Microphone orange dot, circular Charging `%` ring, Focus dot — hidden cleanly when idle) and a right WASAPI peak-reactive artwork-colored music waveform (`IAudioMeterInformation` at `~30 fps` only while playing), brief `320×60` **Now Playing** track banner, plus action-triggered `LumenMascot` (`wave` / `celebrate` / `confirm` / `sleep` eyes & waving hand) over a GPU-composited flowing aurora rim glow (`ActionGlowLayer`, configurable as `On Action`, `Always`, or `Off` in Appearance Settings) that pulses in real time with live system render audio peaks while playing and pauses/zeroes immediately when paused
- **Universal Online + Offline Player Tracking & Artwork Sync:** Hybrid Windows SMTC (`LastUpdatedTime` anchor math at `2 Hz`) + WASAPI `IAudioSessionManager2` / `IAudioMeterInformation` / `EnumWindows` fallback scanner automatically tracking both web browsers and offline desktop video/audio apps (`PotPlayer`, `MPC-HC`/`BE`, `VLC`, `mpv`, `foobar2000`, `AIMP`, `Spotify`, `WMP`) with `WM_APPCOMMAND` + media-key universal control dispatch, `LIVE / PAUSED` indicator in Home `MusicCard` for non-seekable sources, delayed YouTube artwork re-validation, `4:3` black-bar cropping, `MediaGraceStabilizer` zero-flash track-skip holding, and 3D directional side-flip transitions (`Right → Left` Next / `Left → Right` Previous)
- **Screenshot & Offline Screen OCR:** Capture PNG screenshots directly into the **System & Shelf** file list, and extract on-screen text offline via `Windows.Media.Ocr` to clipboard history with 1-click Calendar Hub task creation
- **Apple-Style System Settings:** Pure `#000000` OLED black macOS-inspired dark settings window matching the Notch, featuring traffic light controls, vibrant Apple gradient squircle sidebar icons (`General`, `Appearance`, `Pages`, `Notifications`, `Widgets`, `Plugins`, `About`), grouped preference cards (including **Calendar Hub, Sync & Focus** and BYO OAuth Client IDs), segmented notch scaling (`Auto`, `100%`, `115%`, `130%`, `150%`), toggle switches, and full live IPC synchronization
- **Carousel Slide + Crossfade Page Transitions:** Directional horizontal slide and crossfade animations (`MOTION_PRESETS.pageSwitch`) across **Home**, **Calendar Hub**, **System & Shelf**, and third-party plugins via mouse wheel, keyboard arrows, Home card shortcuts, or page dots
- **Standardized Proportions:** Streamlined resting notch (`184×30`), minimal notch (`110×26`), slim line (`84×12`), unified `640×312` (`r=32`) geometry across all three built-in expanded pages (**Home**, **Calendar Hub**, **System & Shelf**), and `560×172` (`r=24`) secondary plugin shell
- **Hover Auto-Open & Ultra-Slim Notch Return:** Hovering expands directly without any legacy "Click to open" state (`60 ms` intent delay with zero-resize fixed transparent canvas architecture and reflow-free spring morphing), leaving shrinks into an ultra-slim `84×12` notch indicator (`350 ms`), and restores the resting clock pill after `5s` (configurable in Settings)
- **Strict Notch-Bounds Click Pass-Through:** Dynamic 30 Hz hit-testing that sets Win32 `WS_EX_TRANSPARENT` whenever the cursor is outside the physical notch/island boundaries, allowing clicks to pass through completely to background windows, browser tabs, and desktop icons with zero obstruction
- **Zero-Resize Window Architecture:** Windows OS-level window resizing is completely eliminated during hover transitions by maintaining a transparent GPU canvas, enabling 100% silk-smooth 60fps/120fps CSS transform morphing without DWM coordinate lag or cursor displacement
- **Pin Lock (`📌`), Typing Protection & Wheel Paging:** Lock the expanded island open while pinning or typing inside widgets, and scroll the mouse wheel to switch pages
- Auto-hide in true fullscreen apps/games (`FR-WIN-4`), single-instance lock, Windows Startup (`HKCU\...\Run`), System Tray controls, and global hotkeys (`Ctrl+Alt+L`, `Ctrl+Alt+Space`)

### Media & Audio Sync

- Universal Windows SMTC + WASAPI/HWND fallback media controls integrated directly into Home `MusicCard` (play/pause, next/prev, interactive seek or `LIVE / PAUSED` live bar for non-seekable offline players/streams, 3D side-flip transitions, multi-session switcher across browsers & offline apps, mute, and volume slider)
- Album artwork preview with delayed YouTube re-validation, `4:3` letterbox trim, and contrast-guarded dominant accent color extraction (`lumen-media`)
- Live WASAPI peak-reactive waveform (`AudioPeakReader`) & master system volume/mute slider

### Productivity, System & Faith Widgets

- **Unified Tasks, Reminders & Focus (`Calendar Hub`):** Natural quick-add tasks & repeating/prayer-anchored reminders persisted in SQLite (`lumen-storage::calendar_hub`) plus Rust-owned Focus Flow (`lumen-core::focus_engine`) driving Home's `Next` card, `DayArc` event lanes, Focus chip, and resting pill countdown
- **Hydration Card (`Home`):** Compact daily hydration ring (`1500 ml` default), 1-tap `+250 ml` log + long-press `150/250/500 ml` serving picker right on Home (`page 0`)
- **Offline Prayer Times & Auto-Tracked Location (`adhan`):** Auto-tracks Windows Geolocation coordinates (`locationOptIn` enabled by default) to resolve nearest city + host timezone offset cleanly for Weather (`Open-Meteo`), all 5 daily Prayer times, Ramadan Sehri/Iftar countdowns, Qibla bearing (`latitude`/`longitude`), and Day Arc solar positions, with manual Bangladesh city fallback (`Karachi` / `Hanafi`, Dhaka/Khulna & 8 BD cities, English & Bangla)
- **File Shelf, System Telemetry & Clipboard History (`System & Shelf`):** Local file/folder/URL shelf (`IFileOpenDialog` + OS drag-drop + shell open, cap `50`), visibility-gated `1 Hz` CPU/RAM/GPU/Network sparklines, and searchable multi-type Clipboard history with pinning and `30 s` auto-wiped memory-only secrets

### Privacy, Notifications, Settings & SDK

- Privacy-first Notification Toast with swipe-to-dismiss, OTP/PIN/currency keyword masking, allow/deny lists, and Focus Assist quiet respect
- Compact `64 px` System HUDs for Volume, Mute, Caps Lock, and Battery/AC
- Separate on-demand **Settings Window** (General, Appearance, Pages Reorder/Hide, Notifications, Widgets, Plugins, About) + English & Bangla (`বাংলা`) localization + animated **Lumi** mascot
- Sandboxed third-party **Plugin SDK** (`%APPDATA%\Lumen\plugins`, `<iframe sandbox="allow-scripts">`), crash-safe rotating logs (`%APPDATA%\Lumen\logs\lumen.log`), and automated GitHub Release `.exe`/`.msi`/`.zip` builds
